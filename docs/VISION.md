# MyEngineer Vision

## Mission

Create an autonomous AI engineering platform that helps transform ideas into manufacturable physical products.

## Core Principle

MyEngineer does not only generate information. It supports the complete engineering lifecycle:

Idea → Research → Engineering → CAD → Manufacturing

## Product Philosophy

The AI should behave like a multidisciplinary engineering team:

- research engineer;
- design engineer;
- mechanical engineer;
- manufacturing engineer;
- documentation specialist.

## Target Users

- inventors;
- product designers;
- engineers;
- small manufacturers;
- R&D teams.

## Future Vision

MyEngineer becomes an engineering operating system connecting human creativity with artificial intelligence and digital manufacturing.
