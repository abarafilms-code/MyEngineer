# MVP: AI Product Brief Generator

## Goal

First working MyEngineer product.

A user describes an idea and receives a complete product development brief.

## Input

Example:

"Create a professional mobile cleaning system for high-rise buildings"

## Output

### Product Definition
- purpose
- user
- market

### Research
- analogues
- competitors
- existing solutions

### Engineering
- architecture
- components
- materials

### Industrial Design
- style selection
- visual language
- materials
- colors

### Manufacturing
- technology
- estimated production approach
- next CAD steps

This MVP becomes the foundation for future CAD automation.
