# Materials Database

## Purpose

Engineering material knowledge for AI design decisions.

## Categories

### Plastics

- PLA
- PETG
- ABS
- ASA
- PA6/PA12
- PC
- TPU

### Engineering Composites

- carbon fiber reinforced polymers;
- glass fiber reinforced polymers.

### Metals

- aluminum alloys;
- stainless steel;
- titanium alloys.

## AI Analysis Parameters

For every material:

- strength;
- temperature resistance;
- chemical resistance;
- manufacturability;
- cost;
- production method.
