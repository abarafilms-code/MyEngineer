# Engineering Rules

## Design Principles

MyEngineer follows engineering decision logic.

## Rules

- prefer existing solutions before creating new parts;
- use standard components when possible;
- optimize for manufacturability;
- consider maintenance and repair;
- separate purchased and manufactured parts;
- document every engineering decision.

## Design Review

AI evaluates:

- reliability;
- cost;
- complexity;
- innovation potential;
- production feasibility.
